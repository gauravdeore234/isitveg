Privacy Policy — IsItVeg

Last updated: August 19, 2026

IsItVeg is developed by CoSine Labs. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Collection

IsItVeg does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. The app works entirely on your device with no internet connection required.

Camera Usage

The app uses your device camera solely to scan food ingredient labels. Camera images are processed on-device using on-device OCR (optical character recognition) and are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

Scan History

Scan results are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to CoSine Labs or any third party.

Third-Party Services

IsItVeg uses Google ML Kit for on-device text recognition. ML Kit processes data entirely on-device and does not send image data to Google servers. See Google ML Kit Terms for details.

Children's Privacy

IsItVeg does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
